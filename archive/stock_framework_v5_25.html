How the Four-Pillar

THE BIG PICTURE
What is this framework?

Most stock research tools show you one thing — a chart, a P/E ratio, or analyst opinions. This framework combines four completely different lenses into a single weighted composite score from 0 to 10.

The approach is grounded in 2025 academic research on AI-driven stock prediction. The key finding: no single method wins all the time. Technical signals dominate short-term returns; fundamentals matter more long-term; semantic signals catch what numbers miss.

READING THE SCORE
What do the numbers mean?
0–3.9
Strong Sell
4.0–5.4
Sell
5.5–7.1
Neutral
7.2–8.4
Buy
8.5–10
Strong Buy

A score of 5.0 is neutral — the starting point. Every positive signal adds to it; every red flag subtracts. Above 7.0 means the weight of evidence is clearly bullish.

THE FOUR PILLARS
What each pillar measures
◈
Fundamental 30%
Is the business healthy and growing? Looks at financials — P/E, revenue growth, gross margins, ROE — the same metrics a long-term investor studies before buying.
P/E Ratio — How much you pay per dollar of earnings. Under 15x cheap; over 50x expensive.
Revenue Growth — Over 20% YoY is strong; negative is a red flag.
Gross Margin — Over 65% signals exceptional pricing power.
ROE — How efficiently management uses your capital. Over 30% is excellent.
◎
Technical 35% — highest
What is the price doing right now? Reads price behaviour — 52-week range position, moving averages, daily momentum. Strongest short-to-medium-term predictor per research.
52-Week Range — Near highs = momentum; near lows = assess downtrend or value.
Golden Cross — 50-day MA above 200-day MA is a classic buy signal.
Daily Momentum — Today's move vs yesterday and vs the open.
◉
Entropy 10%
How unpredictable and risky is this stock? Measures disorder and volatility. Starts high and subtracts for risk signals. A high-entropy stock can swing 30% in a week.
Beta — 2.0 means the stock moves twice as much as the market.
52W Range Width — Wider range = more entropy.
Sector Complexity — Biotech/crypto have binary outcomes. Utilities are predictable.
◐
Semantic 25%
What does Wall Street think and say? Captures analyst recommendations, price targets, sector narratives. LLM-derived semantic signals add value that pure numbers miss.
Analyst Consensus — 70%+ Buy = strong positive narrative.
Price Target Upside — Mean analyst target vs current price.
Sector Tailwinds — Semiconductors/AI have structural tailwinds in 2025–2026.
How the Composite Score is calculated
Each pillar score (0–10) × its weight. The four are added together.
Technical
35%
Strongest near-term predictor
Fundamental
30%
Long-term value ceiling
Semantic
25%
Forward-looking narrative
Entropy
10%
Risk modifier
Example: Tech 8.0×0.35=2.80 + Fund 7.5×0.30=2.25 + Sem 8.0×0.25=2.00 + Entr 6.0×0.10=0.60
Composite = 7.65 → BUY
